# Privacy Policy for Fight Game **Effective Date:** October 24, 2026 **Application Name:** Fight Game **Package Name:** `com.aistudio.pixelbrawler.vkxyt` **Developer Contact:** sanjib4me123@gmail.com --- ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which **Fight Game** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("Users" or "you") of the **Fight Game** Android application (the "App"). We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy. Fight Game is a 2D retro action fighting game. We do not require registration or personal login credentials, and we do not collect or sell personal identifiable information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number. --- ## 2. Information We Collect and Process ### A. Automatically Collected Data via Third-Party Ad Networks Fight Game integrates **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show in-game banner ads and opt-in rewarded video ads (such as earning coins or match revives). In order to display and optimize advertisements, Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect non-personal diagnostic and advertising data: * **Google Advertising ID (AD_ID / AAID):** Used strictly for advertising purposes and ad performance measurement. * **Device Telemetry:** Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language. * **Approximate Location:** General location derived from IP address at the city/country level (no GPS or fine location is accessed). * **Ad Engagement Metrics:** Impressions, clicks, and ad loading diagnostic logs. ### B. Local Gameplay Data (Stored Exclusively On-Device) All game progress data—such as high scores, unlocked characters, fight records, and earned coins—is stored locally on your device via Android Room database and local storage. This information is **never** uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any private external game servers. --- ## 3. Third-Party Services Our application integrates third-party services provided by Google LLC. These third parties process technical information according to their respective privacy policies: * **Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads SDK:** * [Google Ads Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) * [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) * **Google Play Services:** * [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) --- ## 4. Device Permissions Requested Fight Game requests only standard install-time network permissions necessary for ad delivery: * **`android.permission.INTERNET`:** Allows the App to open network sockets to request and show advertisements. * **`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`:** Allows the App to verify whether an active network connection exists before requesting ads. The App **does not** request, access, or store: * Camera or microphone * GPS or fine/coarse location sensors * Device contacts or phone state * Photos, media, or external storage files * SMS or call logs --- ## 5. User Choices & Advertising Preferences You can manage or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time by configuring your Android device settings: 1. Open your device **Settings**. 2. Go to **Google** > **Ads** (or **Privacy** > **Ads**). 3. Select **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**. --- ## 6. Children's Privacy Fight Game is an arcade fighting game intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The integrated Google AdMob SDK operates in compliance with applicable child protection laws, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately for removal. --- ## 7.
